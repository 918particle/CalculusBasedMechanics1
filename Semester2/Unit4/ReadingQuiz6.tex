\begin{itemize}

\item $\epsilon = -L \Delta I /\Delta t$ ... Faraday's Law
\item 1 H = 1 $\Omega$ s ... The \textit{Henry} is a unit of inductance, often represented by $L$.
\item $\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7}$ ... T m$^{-1}$ A$^{-1}$.

\section{Inductors in Series and Parallel}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Inductors in Series.} Show that the total inductance $L_{\rm tot}$ of two inductors with inductances $L_1$ and $L_2$ in \textit{series} is
\begin{equation}
L_{\rm tot} = L_1 + L_2 \label{eq:1}
\end{equation} 
(a) Draw a diagram below. (b) Is the current the same through both $L_1$ and $L_2$? (c) Write a formula relating the voltage across each inductor and the voltage across both inductors. (d) Substitute Faraday's law to prove Eq. \ref{eq:1}.  \\ \vspace{2.5cm}
\item \textbf{Inductors in Parallel.} Show that the total inductance $L_{\rm tot}$ of two inductors with inductances $L_1$ and $L_2$ in \textit{parallel} is
\begin{equation}
L_{\rm tot}^{-1} = L_1^{-1} + L_2^{-1} \label{eq:2}
\end{equation} 
(a) Draw a diagram below. (b) Write a formula relating the current through each inductor and the total current, and then take the derivative of both sides ($\Delta I/\Delta t$).  (c) Is the voltage the same across both $L_1$ and $L_2$? (d) Substitute Faraday's law to prove Eq. \ref{eq:2}.  \\ \vspace{2.5cm}
\item What rate of current increase will give a back emf of 20 mV for two 100 $\mu$H inductors in series? \\ \vspace{2cm}
\item Camera flashes charge a capacitor to high voltage by switching the current through an inductor on and off rapidly. In what time must the 0.100 A current through a 2.00 mH inductor be switched on or off to induce a 500 V emf? \\ \vspace{1cm}
\end{enumerate}
